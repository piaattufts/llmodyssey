## 0.1.0

First standalone open-source release.

- Thirteen local-first games across Cognitive Core, Systems Forge, and Foundry Arena.
- Shared scoring, three-level hints, mastery threshold, and browser progress.
- Educator view, demo mode with a guided tour, and optional pre/post assessment.
- Optional research adapter and optional model proxy. Neither is required to play.
- Educational simulations are labeled in the interface and in the README.
